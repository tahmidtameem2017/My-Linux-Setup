The music card under the bar — what is playing, plus a player for your own ~/Music. Mod+Alt+M.

How voice typing behaves

It runs entirely on this machine: nothing is uploaded and there is no account. It listens when you ask and stops on a pause, and it types into whichever window you were already using — a terminal, a chat, a document — rather than opening one of its own.

It is a toggle, not hold-to-talkpress once to start, once to stop
Stop when you go quieta pause ends the recording
Know when it is listeningan overlay, plus a dot in the bar
If the dot in the bar lights up for another program, that one is recording toothe dot follows the microphone, not the app

The Mod+Alt+Escape key is the escape hatch. A normal stop is enough almost every time; it exists for the rare moment where the microphone stays open no matter what you press, and it is safe to mash — it touches nothing else on your computer.

Why a file sometimes is not found

Dot-folders and folders ignored by git are never searched — that is why ~/.config and node_modules stay invisible. The list of files is cached; delete ~/.cache/niri-setup/file-index/ to rebuild it after moving a lot of files around.
